## Limits

One dataset, one source (tweets), one language; labels derived from emoticons by the dataset's authors; synthetic noise; no transformers. Subsets of one size overlap, so their spread understates variation across independent training sets. The bootstrap covers test-set sampling only. Differences measured here say nothing about why they occur.
