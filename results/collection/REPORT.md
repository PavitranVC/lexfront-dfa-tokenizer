## Limits

Single seed and one run per cell; the CI covers test-set sampling only. The tuned setting also depends on one random validation split. Datasets differ in size (see section 1); small test sets give wide CIs. Cleaning removes rows, so results on cleaned and raw data are not directly comparable. The five tweet_eval tasks share the same source (tweets), so they are not five independent domains.
